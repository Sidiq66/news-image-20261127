One image that sums up the world news of Friday 27 November 2026, drawn as a newspaper front page. The lead: from Mar-a-Lago, President Trump says Vladimir Putin "would like to make a deal" to end the war in Ukraine, and urges him to pull back military aggression across Europe so a deal can be made quickly. Around it: New Zealand's official election count leaves a hung parliament, with Luxon's coalition one seat short at 65 of 130 and the first-time Opportunity Party holding the balance of power; the Thanksgiving nor'easter keeps battering the US East Coast into Friday, with nearly 900,000 without power and the 100th Macy's parade moved to Saturday — its first weather delay in history; SAP weighs cutting at least 10,000 jobs in an AI-era overhaul of 9% of its workforce; and the Bears stun the Lions 39–35 on a touchdown with three seconds left. Every fact was checked against at least two news sources on the morning of 27 November (ANI, AP, Reuters, Bloomberg, the Electoral Commission among them), and the day's tempting leftovers — Abrego Garcia's departure, the Mayon eruption, the SpaceX Starmind clearance — were left out on purpose: they are earlier days' news, not the 27th's. The page is drawn programmatically by `build_image.py` (PIL, no photographs, no downloaded assets); `main.ts` checks the picture against the gate's limits and places it at the output path as `entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
